# The Caring Cove — Repository Content Strategy Audit

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Audit ID | TCC-AUD-CNT-20260817 |
| Audited baseline | `3b4fc2b68ddf166087fc10f81977e01b0450317d` |
| Scope | Repository only; no competitor, keyword or performance research |
| Verdict | 3/10 content-strategy readiness |

## Current inventory and strengths

- Six service categories, 16 FAQ questions, eight gallery sections, three placeholder blog records and core sales pages.
- Strong latent positioning around boutique, family-centred care for Nairobi and diaspora families.
- Useful founder story, facility gallery, service taxonomy, diaspora FAQ content, tour CTA and centralized `site-content.json`.
- Structured metadata, breadcrumbs and FAQ schema exist.

## Findings

1. Indexed service/team/blog routes contain unrelated template material, fake staff, lorem text, fake comments and IT categories.
2. High-stakes “pain-free,” “hospital-grade,” guaranteed ratio, zero pressure-sore, CQC/GSF, registered nursing and doctor-approved claims lack named evidence/review governance.
3. 1:1/1:4, phone, address, staff and brochure facts conflict.
4. Internal marketing instructions such as addressing fears render as visitor copy.
5. Awareness-stage content fails: no real articles, descriptive slugs, credible authors, reviewers, sources or dates.
6. Consideration coverage is moderate but compressed into pages rather than hub/spoke resources.
7. Decision coverage is weak: prices are hidden, team/proof are unreliable and the care-guide CTA is broken.
8. Admissions coverage is thin: no verified assessment process, eligibility boundaries, required information, next steps or response expectations.
9. No genuine case studies, customer research, sales objections, keyword evidence, competitor gap analysis, performance data or editorial workflow exists.
10. Search, comments, categories, consultation forms and social `#` links are nonfunctional.
11. The brochure is image-only/untagged and contains stale Alyaa Mufid, Day Centre, phone/address and service information requiring confirmation.
12. No content owners, publication states, claim evidence, style guide, review cadence, expiry or withdrawal mechanism exists.

## Proposed pillars requiring evidence and owner approval

1. Memory and dementia care.
2. Palliative and complex nursing care.
3. Rehabilitation, convalescence and respite.
4. Boutique senior living, safety and daily life.
5. Diaspora family guidance and care transparency.

## Required disposition

Quarantine fake content; establish canonical facts, claims and publication controls; repair the core decision journey and admissions information; rebuild or withdraw the brochure; create research/editorial governance; launch blog routes only after a useful, sourced, reviewed minimum set exists.
